# Specialist Module Standards

Load only the relevant domain section mentally; do not expand every module for unrelated work.

## Music
Preserve approved current visuals. Aim for frictionless playback and library UX: persistent player, player/track context actions, instant Add to Playlist, inline playlist creation, queue/play-next/reorder, likes, history, artist follows, album/artist depth, search and real-signal discovery. Reuse shared engagement/search/notification systems.

## Community / Forum
Target mature community-suite capability depth comparable to products such as Invision Community, without cloning visual/code. Forums/subforums, topics/replies, Q&A, rich editor/drafts, mentions, quotes/multi-quote, polls, reactions, reputation/ranks, bookmarks, follows, unread tracking, activity streams, tags, featured/trending, granular permissions, ModCP, reports, moderation, warnings/restrictions, spam controls, clubs/groups, shared messaging, member privacy and search.

## Cinema
Preserve approved visuals. Canonical Movies/Series/Seasons/Episodes/People, watch states, episode progress, ratings/reviews, spoilers, lists, cast/crew, media, related titles and cross-links to Magazine/Community/Store.

## Games
Preserve approved visuals. Games/Editions/Platforms, release metadata, requirements, wishlist/library state, ratings/reviews, media, guides/news, community links and commerce relationships.

## Magazine
Preserve approved visuals. Editorial workflow, author identities, revisions, scheduling, article series, bookmarks, reader UX, canonical cross-links and SEO.

## Store
Catalog/variants/cart/checkout/orders/inventory/fulfillment, reviews/Q&A, discounts, wishlists/comparison where meaningful, secure digital delivery, payment idempotency and concurrency-safe inventory.

## Support
Tickets/categories/priorities/status/assignment/internal notes/attachments with references to users/orders/services/projects and full audit.

## Hosting
Provider adapters, plans/services/provisioning lifecycle/usage/renewals/invoices/status/support. Infrastructure secrets remain secret.

## AI
Provider/model abstraction, quotas/access/settings/health. No provider keys in UI/logs.

## Events / Gallery / Downloads
Reusable capabilities that other modules can mount/reference rather than reimplement.
