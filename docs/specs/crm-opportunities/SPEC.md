# مدیریت فرصت‌های فروش

An existing customer has one or more tracked CRM sales opportunities. A staff member may explicitly convert a source form submission or ticket into one opportunity after accessing the source through its owner. A source can be reused as a link by its authorized viewers. The same source creates at most one opportunity. For an anonymous form, the operator explicitly selects an existing customer; no account is created. CRM is the owner of the opportunity and its history. Identity remains the owner of customers, Forms and Support own records and scopes, and the existing follow-up/Operations/Notifications stack owns tasks and internal reminders.

The same current CRM permissions admit the private desk. Holders see their opportunities. Staff with `users.manage` or full access can view and assign all, including work without a holder. Source conversion additionally enforces the source's assigned work/module/department rules. Names and source content are not copied into the revision/audit metadata. Source links are resolved under current source permissions. A customer erasure removes private opportunities and history, and staff erasure removes staff references. Module deactivation hides the API, preserves data, and suppresses reminders.

New work starts in NEW, can progress between the three open stages NEW, QUALIFIED and PROPOSAL, and can close WON or LOST. LOST requires a short reason. A closed opportunity retains its history. Updates compare the observed version. The change, transition, closure of open linked follow-ups and strict audit share one transaction. Closing follow-ups increments their versions. A linked task reuses the existing due day and holder; opportunity reassignment leaves explicitly assigned tasks with their holders. Each linked task can deliver one internal reminder for the same assignee and due day from 09:00 Tehran time. Reminders whose jobs execute while the module is disabled are skipped. No external notice or order is created.

`/admin/crm/opportunities` offers a Persian RTL stage view with scoped owner/stage filters, creation, detail and fixed status semantics. Board summary applies its customer and owner scopes: current stage counts, success percentage among closed rows, 14-day open opportunities without a stage change, and opportunity amounts for WON grouped independently by currency in minor units. Amounts are estimates and do not affect finance, checkout or payments. Versioned commands use the existing Origin and private cache policies. Loading, empty, refusal, stale edits and conflicts are visible to the operator.

An additive schema migration retains existing files, customers, forms, tickets and tasks. Accept with the registered no-provider PostgreSQL test for conversion races, forged source/owner scopes, stage audit and history, exact currency, reminders, erasure, module-off and the empty/populated migration paths. Exercise the real Persian controls at desktop and mobile in light and dark. Roll back application and keep data; the private erasure hooks must keep running for the new rows.
